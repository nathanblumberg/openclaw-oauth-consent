Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

OpenClaw Email Assistant is a personal email-assistance application used only by authorized users.

Information accessed

When an authorized user connects a Google account, the application may access email information and account-identifying information necessary to provide the features the user requests. Access is limited to the permissions shown during Google authorization.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and maintain the requested email-assistance functions. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the service, comply with law, or protect security.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials and application data are stored on systems controlled by the application owner. Reasonable safeguards are used to restrict access. Users can revoke Google access at any time from their Google Account permissions.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate the application. An authorized user may request deletion of stored application data or revoke access through Google.
